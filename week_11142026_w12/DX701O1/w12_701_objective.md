Week 12 Overview
In today’s globalized tech industry, regulatory frameworks play a pivotal role in shaping how companies operate, innovate, and manage data. This week, we will explore key regulations, including the U.S. government’s national security-driven policies and the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Digital Markets Act (DMA), and how they have influenced major tech companies. Through case studies, you will analyze how these regulatory frameworks are developed and enforced and their lasting impacts on global operations. 


Learning Objectives 
At the end of this week, you will be able to: 

Identify key regulation impacting global tech companies 
Describe the elements of regulations, including scope and enforcement 
Discuss the consequences and impacts of regulation

Required Resources
The following resources are required for your learning this week. Make sure to read two articles, as you may be tested on the concepts in an upcoming assessment.  

Reading | Laybats, C., & Davies, J. (2018). GDPR: Implementing the regulations. Business Information Review, 35(2), 81–83. 

This article explores the implications of the GDPR for organizations within and outside the EU, emphasizing key changes such as the requirement for explicit consent, the right to be forgotten, and restrictions on automated decision-making processes. The article also includes an insightful interview with John Davies, managing director of a digital agency, who shares practical perspectives on how organizations are adapting to the new regulations. 

Reading | European Council. (n.d.). What is GDPR, the EU’s new data protection law? 

This article provides a clear and concise overview of the GDPR, highlighting its key principles: data subject rights, consent, data protection by design, accountability and transparency, and penalties for non-compliance. 

Resource | GDPR Info. (n.d.). General data protection regulation (GDPR). 

The website GDPR Info provides the full legal text of the GDPR, organized into chapters and articles, along with detailed explanations and commentary for each provision. For data science professionals, this is an essential resource to learn how data privacy laws impact data collection, processing, and analysis, ensuring ethical and compliant practices in their future careers.
