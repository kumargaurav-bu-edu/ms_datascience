Recommended Milestone 2 workflow
1. Establish the research problem
   
   Revisit your original Milestone 1 project, research question, business problem, and dataset. Make sure every model contributes to answering the same central problem.
2. Audit Weeks 8–11 notebooks
   
   Review every notebook and extract actual results, metrics, model configurations, tuning results, and observations.
3. Identify analytical gaps
   
   Ask AI to identify missing experiments, weak comparisons, unsupported interpretations, and opportunities for deeper analysis.
4. Select two deep-dive topics
   
   Choose the two topics with the strongest evidence and most meaningful insights. Still discuss all four weeks.
5. Build the evidence and conclusion matrix
   
   Connect every research question to model results, quantitative evidence, implications, and limitations.
6. Draft the report
   
   Write the report only after the technical analysis and evidence are complete.
7. Run a strict rubric audit
   
   Evaluate all nine criteria. Revise until each one has evidence supporting an Exemplary assessment.
