Week 4 Overview
Building on last week’s discussion of foundational theories about ethical issues, we now turn our focus to critical thinking and effective reasoning in cyberethics. We will analyze the structure of an argument, distinguish between premises and conclusions, and assess the validity of reasoning. Through videos, readings, and activities, we will explore key concepts such as logical fallacies, their impact on arguments, and the ethical and technological challenges surrounding AI systems. This week, you will also begin preparing for your case study assignment by reviewing sources from your policy analysis proposal and analyzing a specific ethical issue in technology. 




Learning Objectives
At the end of this week, you will be able to:

Identify the structure of an argument, including premise and conclusion.  
Determine whether an argument is valid or fallacious in its reasoning.   
Develop and defend a persuasive argument on an ethical technology issue using appropriate ethical frameworks.
