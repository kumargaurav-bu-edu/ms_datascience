Week 4 Tasks
After completing your policy analysis proposal, the next step is to analyze a case study that illustrates the problem you aim to solve. Of the events included in your proposal, pick the one that has more sources and enough complexity to warrant a more robust analysis.  

Your case study must include the following: 

A name for the case study (which you create). 
A topic area. 
A summary of the events of the case (250–300 words). 
A list of stakeholders who were impacted in the case study, including a sentence or two with explanations of each stakeholder's connection to the case. Be sure to list at least three to five stakeholders (250–300 words). 
An explanation of how the case study is an example of broader issues in technology and ethics, including references to related case studies (if possible) (250–300 words). 
An argument about why this case study is worth resolving (150–200 words).
Please note: Your case study should be between 900 and 1,100 words.
