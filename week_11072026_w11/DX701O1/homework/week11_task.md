Building a Policy Analysis - Week 11 Tasks
A key part of your proposal is addressing potential critiques of your policy. Since you have examined other policies in your impact area, you may already be aware of some critiques that could also apply to your policy. This week, consider the following questions:  

Identify the critiques: What are the most significant critiques of your proposed policy?  
Evaluate their relevance: Which critiques are most relevant to the implementation of your policy?  
Develop your rebuttal: How can you respond to these critiques? How does your policy anticipate and address them?  
Remember, ethics involve the process of persuasion. Addressing potential criticisms makes your policy more persuasive. Be thoughtful and thorough in considering how to strengthen your proposal by responding to potential challenges. 

In your analysis, focus on the critiques you believe are most significant. Consider, for example, that a policy addressed by companies might face some initial resistance from them. What arguments can you use to overcome the objections that companies might have to your policy?
