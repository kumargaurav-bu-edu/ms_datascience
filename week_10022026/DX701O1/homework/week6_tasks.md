Week 6 Tasks
This week, you will focus on crafting the summary section of your case study, which will serve as a critical foundation for your policy analysis. The summary provides an opportunity to clearly present the key events of your chosen case and lays the groundwork for the more detailed analysis that follows. 

Your case study should illustrate a specific instance of the broader issue you are addressing in your policy analysis. A well-crafted summary highlights the most important aspects of the case, offering readers the context they need to understand its significance. 

Keep your summary concise, capturing the essential details in 250–300 words. Once complete, be sure to add the summary to the case study template you have been working on, keeping it organized with the other sections. 

To get started, consider this question: How can you clearly and concisely explain the main events and stakeholders? The example summary and the corresponding analysis below will guide you in completing this task. 

Example Summary 
Cambridge Analytica, a British consulting firm, took advantage of an exploit within Facebook’s system to harvest data from not only the people who used their app but also from their friends. Cambridge Analytica used data harvested from the exploit to build psychometric profiles on each person in the dataset, attempting to determine each person’s political preferences. These profiles were then used to determine which voters would be most likely to respond to attempts at political persuasion, including the type of news content (or fake news content) that might be the most persuasive. Cambridge Analytica’s activities came to public light after whistleblower Christopher Wylie came forward. 

Analysis 
This summary looks to highlight the two most important actors in the case: Cambridge Analytica and Facebook. Other stakeholders may include those affected by the hack, people who may have looked to use Cambridge Analytica’s services, and the whistleblower, Christopher Wylie. The categories that you have been assigned to fill out should also serve as potential hints at what elements are most important in any given case. While you will provide greater detail on each of these elements, as is the case with the stakeholders, the summary is a great opportunity to show how each of those elements fit together.
