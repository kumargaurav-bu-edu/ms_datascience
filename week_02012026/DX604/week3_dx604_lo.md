- Topic Overview 
In this section, we explore the role of data schemas in organizing and structuring databases. We begin by learning about the different types of schemas — conceptual, logical, and physical—and their specific functions. Next, we look at best practices for designing effective schemas. Finally, we examine the challenges of schema evolution in dynamic environments and how to adapt schemas as data needs change.

  Topic Overview 
In this section, we examine types of data governance, focusing on key frameworks that guide data management. We cover centralized data governance, which consolidates authority for consistent policies, and decentralized data governance, which distributes authority for flexibility and local responsiveness. Additionally, we highlight the role of regulatory compliance in ensuring that effective governance frameworks help organizations meet legal and industry standards. These elements form a comprehensive approach to leveraging data effectively and responsibly.

- Topic Overview 
This section provides an overview of implementing data governance, highlighting key components for success. We discuss forming a data governance council, including best practices for selecting members and defining roles. We explore the development of data governance policies and standards, as well as the importance of data stewardship and quality management in maintaining data integrity. Finally, we review the technologies and tools that support data governance initiatives, equipping organizations to effectively implement and sustain their governance efforts.
