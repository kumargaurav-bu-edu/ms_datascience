Week 5 Tasks
You have started your case study in Week 4. After reviewing the sources from your proposal, your task now is to go over the case you chose and identify key stakeholders. Briefly explain each stakeholder's connection to the case and their role or perspective. Follow these steps to guide your analysis:

Step 1: Identify Stakeholders: 
Consider individuals, groups, or entities affected by or involved in the case. For example, in the Uber case study, you could ask yourself: 

Who is the victim? Would this person have been a customer? A bystander?
Is there a perpetrator? 
Is a company involved? If so, who represents the company (e.g., employees, management)? 
Are there other people connected to the victim or the case? 
Step 2: Explain Roles: 
For each stakeholder, describe their role and significance. Use specific examples from your case. For example, in our Uber case study, stakeholders included: 

The crash victim: Elaine Herzberg 
Family members: Christine Paredes (daughter) and Sheila Wood (sister) 
Uber representatives: Dara Khosrowshahi (CEO) and Jane Smith (low-level employee) 
Safety driver: Rafaela Vasquez 
Local authorities: Maricopa County District Attorney 
Government: the State of Arizona, which authorized the testing of self-driving vehicles
