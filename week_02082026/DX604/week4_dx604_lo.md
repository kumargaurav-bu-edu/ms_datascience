- Week 4 Overview
This week, we will cover foundational concepts and practical approaches for ensuring high-quality and secure data within organizations. We begin with an exploration of data quality—its significance, key dimensions, and common challenges in maintaining it. We then delve into data governance as a means to sustain data quality and explore techniques like data profiling, cleansing, and automation to improve data accuracy and reliability. Then, we shift to master data management (MDM), its architecture, and its role in unifying and governing critical data assets. We also address the practicalities of MDM implementation, the selection of MDM tools, and the potential of cloud-based MDM, capped with real-world examples that highlight best practices and challenges.




- Learning Objectives  
At the end of this week, you will be able to: 

Define data quality and its dimensions  
Implement data quality improvement techniques  
Explore the principles of master data management (MDM) 
Apply MDM tools and practices
