Week 9 Overview
This week, we focus on cybersecurity and how cyberspace vulnerabilities are created and exploited. In a case study, we'll investigate key threats, ethical dilemmas, and real-world consequences of weak security. We'll also examine the responsibilities of individuals and organizations in protecting data and maintaining trust.


Learning Objectives
At the end of this week, you should be able to: Define cybercrime and cyber-related crimes. 
Describe the elements of cybersecurity. 
Identify tradeoffs between data privacy and cybersecurity. 
Analyze the dangers of large-scale data breaches. 

Required Resources
The following resources are required for your learning this week. Make sure you review everything linked below, as you may be tested on the concepts in an upcoming Quiz. 

Read |Tavani, H. T. (2024). Chapter 6: Security and cyberspace. In Ethics and technology: Controversies, questions, and strategies for ethical computing (5th ed.). Wiley. 

This chapter explores cybersecurity issues, including whether cyber intrusions can be ethically justified and if government agencies in sovereign nations might ever be morally permitted to conduct cyberattacks or computer break-ins.

Read |Tavani, H. T. (2024). Chapter 7: Cybercrime and Cyber‐Related Crimes. In Ethics and technology: Controversies, questions, and strategies for ethical computing (5th ed.). Wiley. 

The previous chapter explores cybersecurity issues, with a focus on tensions between security and privacy. This chapter shifts its focus to criminal activities involving cybertechnology.
