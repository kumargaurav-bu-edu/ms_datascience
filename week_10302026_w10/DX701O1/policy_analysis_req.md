Policy Analysis Requirements

Note that the policy analysis paper is due at the end of Week 13 on Sunday, November 29 at 11:59 PM ET.



The following are the requirements for the policy analysis, in accordance with the Policy Analysis rubric (which is exactly how we will be grading this assignment) and compatible with the Policy Analysis template placed in the Week 8 materials, as well as the Policy Analysis Guide, the link to which was sent via email announcement and the associated announcement is available in the "Announcements" section of Blackboard as "Policy Analysis Guide (Weeks 8 - 13 Building A Policy Analysis Tasks)".

The page length of your Policy Analysis MUST be 8-10 pages double-spaced OR 4-5 pages single-spaced, depending on your preference. Title, references/works cited, and appendix pages do NOT count towards the page count.
Each section must be done within the guidelines of the Policy Analysis assignment as stated in the Policy Analysis Rubric available in the Week 8 materials.
Cite your sources using in-text citations. Graduate-level papers are always expected to integrate in-text citations, to some degree, and a references/works cited section.
Ensure that all references in your references/works cited section are cited in your policy analysis paper.
Ensure that you have at least 12 unique bodies of work in the references/works cited section of your Policy Analysis paper that can serve as relevant and credible sources to support your arguments. This does not mean that every single news article, academic journal article, book, video, etc. that you cite must be from a unique publication but, rather, you need to cite 12 unique news articles, journal articles, books, videos, etc. However, source diversity will increase the quality of your overall argument as you integrate unique points of view.
Do NOT forget to add the AI appendix in accordance with the rubric requirements.




Policy Analysis Tips

Now that you all have your case study grades, we also have some additional advice for improving your policy analysis, partially inspired by inquiries that have been made on Yellowdig:

We recommend that you use the Policy Analysis Template available in the Week 8 materials to structure your paper with the References and AI appendix sections after the contents of your paper.
If you will not be using the Policy Analysis template outright, then we recommend that you use APA format.
Formatting your paper using either the Policy Analysis template or APA format will ensure that assessing for details, quality, clarity, and overall grading can be done more seamlessly by the learning facilitators involved in grading your final paper. This is to your benefit.
Using your judgment, you may integrate parts of your Policy Proposal and/or Case Study into your Policy Analysis paper so long as you include relevant citations, ensure that any relevant sources are included in your references/works cited section, and those details are specifically relevant to the section in which you will be including these details. However, your previous papers DO NOT count as references in the references/works cited section of your Policy Analysis.
