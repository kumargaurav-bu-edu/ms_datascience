Week 7 Overview
This week, we’ll examine the ethical and social dimensions of online communities, starting with how these platforms enable global connections based on shared interests. Next, we’ll address content moderation and discuss both human and automated approaches. We’ll conclude with a case study on how Nextdoor’s design changes reduced racial profiling, illustrating the power of technology to shape user behavior and promote fairness. Please note that by the end of this week, you will submit your case study for the Policy Analysis, a key component of the final project.

Learning Objectives
At the end of this week, you should be able to:

Explain the social challenges unique to virtual communities and communities at scale 
Describe the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in social networks, community management, and content moderation 
Identify instances of prejudice, bias, and stereotyping

Required Resources
The following resources are required for your learning this week. Make sure to read two articles, as you may be tested on the concepts in an upcoming assessment. 

Reading | Simon, P. (2018, May 11). How Nextdoor addressed racial profiling on its platform. Harvard Business Review.

This Harvard Business Review article examines how Nextdoor, a neighborhood-focused social network, tackled racial profiling in its crime and safety forums. It highlights how user interface modifications can influence behavior and reduce bias and emphasizes the need for responsible data practices that promote inclusivity and fairness. 

Reading | Hill, K. (2016, August 25). How Nextdoor reduced racist posts by 75%. Splinter. 

This article underscores the importance of ethical considerations in platform design and data collection. It highlights how user interface modifications can influence behavior and reduce bias, emphasizing the need for responsible data practices that promote inclusivity and fairness.  

Reading | Nextdoor. (n.d.). Prevent racial profiling on Nextdoor. Nextdoor Help Center. 

This Nextdoor Help Center article outlines the platform's commitment to eliminating racial profiling in its community interactions. It defines racial profiling as assuming someone is suspicious based on race or ethnicity or providing vague descriptions that cast suspicion on an entire group. The article also explains several measures implemented to influence user behavior, reduce discrimination, and promote inclusivity.
