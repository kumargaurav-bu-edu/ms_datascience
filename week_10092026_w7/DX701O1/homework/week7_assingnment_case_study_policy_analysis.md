Case Study

Your case study must include the following: 

A name for the case study (which you create). 
A topic area. 
Your case study must also include the following sections:
"Case Study Summary" section ("Summary of the Case" section in the template): A summary of the events of the case (250–300 words). 
"Stakeholders and Impact" section ("Stakeholders" section in the template): A list of stakeholders who were impacted in the case study, including a sentence or two with explanations of each stakeholder's connection to the case. Be sure to list at least three to five stakeholders (250–300 words). 
"Broader Issues in Technology and Ethics" section ("Connection to Broader Issues" section in the template): An explanation of how the case study is an example of broader issues in technology and ethics, including references to related case studies (if possible) (250–300 words). 
"Argument for Importance" section ("Importance" section in the template): An argument about why this case study is worth resolving (150–200 words).

AI Appendix

Additionally, your Appendix on AI use (AI Appendix section in the template) must contain the following points:

(a) full exchanges with AI tools,
(b) identification of tools used,
(c) descriptions of how and why they were used,
and (d) thoughtful reflection on impact.
Proper credit for AI usage is given, and work demonstrates critical engagement.

Suggested Workflow

Focus on a single case for the case study as your goal is to elaborate on a specific case only. Additionally, you will want to ensure that this case is the same one that you want to work on for your policy analysis. While it is not a strict requirement for you to use the same exact case for both papers, it is highly advised that you stick to one case for both papers for consistency and to lessen your necessary workload.

Use the provided assignment instructions from Week 4 (which are also featured here) to answer the section prompts to the best of your ability for the 4 identified content sections and outline your AI use in the AI appendix (remember the proper formatting).

Using the rubric provided in the Week 4 "Building a Policy Analysis" content, read through the rubric items for each content section and the AI appendix and evaluate your section content accordingly.

Refine your section content and AI appendix as needed until you have answered the section prompts to the best of your ability.


What case do you use?

It is highly recommended that you stick to a case that you used as an example of the problem in the policy proposal. This is because your policy proposal is partially graded based on the quality of sources that you have found and the validity and relevance of the cases to your problem statement.

However, you may do the case study on a different case/example of the problem, but you must ensure that it meets the same quality standards as required for the case study.

There is no guarantee that a new case will be appropriate until grading for the case study occurs, whereas the examples of the problem will already be vetted.
