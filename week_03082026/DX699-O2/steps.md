One thing that has helped me while working on assignments and projects is following a simple checklist before jumping into modeling:

Understand the dataset – Look at variable types, missing values, and distributions.

Define the question clearly – What exactly are you trying to predict or analyze?

Start with simple analysis – Use univariate and bivariate exploration first. Create a baseline

model – Begin with something simple before trying complex models. Evaluate results

carefully – Check if the model actually answers your research question.
