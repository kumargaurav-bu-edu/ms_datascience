Learning Objectives
At the end of this week, you should be able to:

Explain representation
Explain bag of words
Explain term frequency-inverse document frequency (TF-IDF)
Explain n-grams
Describe named entity extraction

Think About It
Is it okay that bag of words doesn’t keep track of word order? What problems could that cause?
In TF-IDF, why does the IDF get bigger when there are fewer instances of the given word? Why is that a good thing for the algorithm?
N-grams uses word pairs. How long do you think a document has to be before the word pairs will usually repeat? If the word pairs never repeat, is that okay?
