Key Terms

Predictive model: a way of guessing or predicting a target or label based on features or attributes
Target or label: the information or data you want to guess or predict
Features or attributes: the information or data that helps you make the guess or prediction
Informative attribute: the feature or attribute that provides a predictive model with the most helpful information for making its prediction
Entropy: A number that reflects the number of options in a given choice. Each additional choice increases the entropy, but an unlikely choice increases it less than a likely choice.
Information gain: The change in entropy caused by a branch or choice. For example, creating a model that knows that roses are red and violets are blue would cause some information gain.
