For this week, you should research all of the currently proposed solutions. For example, after the release of ChatGPT, several universities, companies, and governments created documents on how they would handle generative AI within their organizations. These solutions can serve as examples of what people are already thinking about on your given topic. It would be even better if you found proposed solutions in your home country or state. Remember that these proposals likely use special terminology to talk about the problem, which, if you research those, will help you to find other resources.  

Once you have gathered these proposals, describe and evaluate each one. Consider these questions: 

What are the strengths and weaknesses of each proposal?
Are there elements of these proposals that you might add, remove, or potentially combine?
You can use these proposals as a baseline for your final policy advocacy. You do not need to adopt anything from other policies, but your paper will be more persuasive if you analyze them.
